TITLE
Snowstorm at the Stargazer's Cabin | 2 Hours of Crackling Fire, Wind & Distant Thunder for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
You're warm. You're safe. Let the storm stay outside.

Snow drifts past the big arched window. The fire crackles in the stone hearth, the lanterns glow, and far away over the mountains, thunder rolls. Sink into the armchair, pull the blanket up, and let two hours of steady, gentle sound carry you off to sleep.

What you'll hear:
• A crackling fireplace
• Soft winter wind against the cabin
• Distant thunder that stays far away
• Quiet piano and warm pads underneath

Good for sleep, reading, studying, or just giving your mind somewhere calm to rest.

Close your eyes. The cabin will keep watch.
