# Autumn Rain – Mr. Fox (3-part short)

Vertical 1080x1920 shorts cut from the `glasshouse/` scene on branch `claude/focused-curie-orkd4f` (its loop video, rain ambience and lo-fi music), with narration and centred captions.

`tools/` holds the scripts: `story.py` (script), `tts.py` (narration, Kokoro `af_heart` voice), `build.py` (pixelated HD-2D look, pan, captions, audio mix). They expect that branch checked out at `src/`, the Kokoro model files in `tts/` and Pixelify Sans in `fonts/`.


## Part 1 – Mr. Fox

You place your umbrella on the counter and let out a sigh, brushing off the autumn leaf that clung to your coat through a long, dreadful day. As the lights flicker on, you realize Mr. Fox got to the armchair first. As usual. You chuckle. Soon there will be hot tea, a warm armchair to curl up in, and that chapter you left off yesterday.


## Part 2 – The Cottage

Your cousins snickered when your grandmother left you her cottage in her will. “It’s not like anyone would want to live in that tattered little shack anyway.” What they didn’t know is that it may look tattered at first sight, but it is by no means little, nor is it just a shack. Since you work from home most days, you restored the beautiful Victorian cottage into a clean, modern, comfortable home. It’s far cozier than the shoebox apartments you’d lived in ever since you started working. And the corner you love most is the glasshouse at the back of the house.


## Part 3 – The Glasshouse

With a heater and proper waterproofing installed, the glasshouse became a gorgeous place to unwind with your favorite books. Every evening, you sit here with a cup of hot tea, easing your nerves after a stressful day. One autumn afternoon, an auburn fox came to visit. He must have been looking for a warm place to escape the harsh autumn wind. You let him in. Ever since, you’ve been good friends. He comes and goes through the little door you installed just for him. What an adorable companion.
