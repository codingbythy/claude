TACTICAL DUEL - HOW TO PLAY

MOVEMENT: Click your soldier, then click a valid square (up to 2 squares away). All 8 directions allowed.
ATTACK: If enemy is within 3 squares, click ATTACK to deal 1 damage. Cannot move after attacking.
FORTIFY: Gain a defense token that blocks the next attack. Cannot stack multiple tokens.
VICTORY: Eliminate enemy (reduce to 0 HP) OR occupy center at full HP (3 HP).
TIMER: You have 20 seconds per turn. Timeout = forfeit!
SUDDEN DEATH: After turn 15, center victory no longer requires full HP.
