TITLE
(NO ADS) Two Foxes Asleep at the Snowed-In Station ❄️ Cozy Lo-Fi, Wood Stove & Blizzard

DESCRIPTION
2 hours of mellow lo-fi in a warm station waiting room while a blizzard blows outside, with the wood stove, the clock and the wind underneath, for studying, reading or winding down.

The last train left hours ago and the line is snowed in. The stationmaster banked up the stove, left the lamp burning, and let two foxes in out of the blizzard.
The storm can have the platform. In here it's warm.

What you will hear, for 2 hours:
• Mellow lo-fi at an unhurried 68 BPM that keeps changing, with no loop
• Blizzard wind in long gusts, underneath the music
• A warm, low crackling fire
• The waiting-room clock

Made for studying, reading, writing, or waiting out a long evening somewhere warm.
