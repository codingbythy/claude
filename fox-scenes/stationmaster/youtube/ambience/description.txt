DESCRIPTION
3 hours of crackling fireplace sounds in a snowed-in mountain station, with the blizzard muffled behind the timber walls. No music and no voices: just the fire, the storm outside and the slow tick of the clock, for deep sleep, reading, or a quiet winter night in.

The last train left hours ago and the line is snowed in. The stationmaster has banked up the stove, pulled the armchair up to the window, made a mug of hot chocolate and left the lamp burning for anyone who needs to come in out of the blizzard.
The storm can have the platform. In here it's warm.

What you will hear, for 3 hours:
• A warm, low crackling fire, close by
• The blizzard outside, muffled by the timber walls: a low roar that rises and falls in long gusts
• Now and then a gust thumps the wall or the wind moans in the eaves
• The waiting-room clock, ticking softly
• No music, no voices

Pull the blanket up, let the fire crackle, and let the storm outside slowly quiet your mind. Made to leave playing while you drift off, read, or wait out a long winter night somewhere warm.
