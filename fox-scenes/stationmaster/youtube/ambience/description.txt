TITLE
Blizzard at the Stationmaster's Waiting Room | Howling Wind & Wood Stove Sounds for Sleep | 2 Hours

DESCRIPTION
2 hours of blizzard sounds heard from a warm station waiting room: howling wind, a crackling wood stove and a ticking clock, with no music, for sleep, study or rest.

The last train left hours ago and the line is snowed in. The stationmaster has banked up the stove, put the kettle on and left the lamp burning for anyone who needs to come in out of the blizzard.
The storm can have the platform. In here it's warm.

What you will hear, for 2 hours:
• Blizzard wind that rises and falls in long gusts
• A cast-iron stove, crackling and ticking as it warms
• The waiting-room clock
• Snow ticking against the glass
• Now and then a gust thumps the wall, whistles in the window frame, or a log shifts in the stove
• No music

Good for sleep, reading, studying, or giving your mind somewhere calm to wait out the weather.
