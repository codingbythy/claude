The last train left hours ago and the line is snowed in. The stationmaster banked up the stove, left the lamp burning, and let two foxes in out of the blizzard.
The storm can have the platform. In here it's warm.

What you will hear, for 2 hours:
• Blizzard wind that rises and falls in long gusts
• A cast-iron stove, crackling and ticking as it warms
• The waiting-room clock
• Snow ticking against the glass
