# Project rules

- ElevenLabs is for text to speech only. Never use it for image generation or music generation.
- Paintings and other images are made in ChatGPT (Sora) or with Google Veo. If neither is reachable from the session, write the
  prompt and ask for the image instead of generating it elsewhere.
- The three live videos (`cozy-cabin/`, `lighthouse-room/`, `glasshouse/`) are left as they are. New episodes live in `fox-scenes/`.
- Videos are 2 hours long.
- Two formats, both with the two foxes: lo-fi (music over the scene's own sounds) and ambience (the scene's sounds only, no
  music). The effort goes into the setting, the title and the soundscape: a detailed, immersive place, a title that names it,
  and sounds that belong to that place.
