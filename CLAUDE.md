# Project rules

- ElevenLabs is for text to speech only. Never use it for image generation or music generation.
- Paintings and other images are made in ChatGPT (Sora) or with Google Veo. If neither is reachable from the session, write the
  prompt and ask for the image instead of generating it elsewhere.
- The three live videos (`cozy-cabin/`, `lighthouse-room/`, `glasshouse/`) are left as they are. New episodes live in `fox-scenes/`.
- Videos are 2 hours long.
- Two formats: lo-fi (music over the scene's own sounds, with the two foxes) and ambience (the scene's sounds only, no
  music). The stationmaster ambience shows the setting alone, without the foxes, opens with snow blowing clear and carries the
  "Somewhere Sheltered" mark in the bottom right-hand corner. The effort goes into the setting, the title and the soundscape: a
  detailed, immersive place, a title that names it, and sounds that belong to that place and vary over the whole video.
- Lo-fi music is made with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT; `fox-scenes/make-music.ipynb`). Free plans of other music generators forbid
  commercial use.
