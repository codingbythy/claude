# Project rules

- ElevenLabs is for text to speech only. Never use it for image generation or music generation.
- Paintings and other images are made in ChatGPT (Sora) or with Google Veo. If neither is reachable from the session, write the
  prompt and ask for the image instead of generating it elsewhere.
- The three live videos (`cozy-cabin/`, `lighthouse-room/`, `glasshouse/`) are left as they are. New episodes live in `fox-scenes/`.
- Lo-fi videos are 2 hours long; ambience videos are 3 hours (may go longer later: sleep videos often run 8 to 10).
- Two formats: lo-fi (music over the scene's own sounds, with the two foxes) and ambience (the scene's sounds only, no
  music). Ambience is one series: blizzard and fireplace, each video in a different sheltered place. The fire is in front,
  crackling; the blizzard is heard muffled, as if through the walls. The stationmaster ambience shows the setting alone, without the foxes, and opens with snow blowing clear. The
  "Somewhere Sheltered" mark is hidden in the scene, slightly see-through (there, a maker's mark on the suitcase), never laid
  over the corner. Thumbnails never cover the hero detail (the hot chocolate). The effort goes into the setting, the title and the soundscape: a
  detailed, immersive place, a title that names it, and sounds that belong to that place and vary over the whole video.
- The fire is myNoise's Fireplace on its Warm and Cosy setting, used with the owner's permission. Its sound files never go
  into this repository (it is public): they live in the channel's Google Drive (Cozy Scenes / fire-stems) and the build
  reads them from there. Descriptions credit it: "Fireplace sound: myNoise.net, used with permission."
- Lo-fi music is made with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT; `fox-scenes/make-music.ipynb`). Free plans of other music generators forbid
  commercial use.
